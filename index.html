Yuze Xia 夏语泽

Welcome to my website! I am a PhD candidate in Accounting at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University. My research examines how financial institutions generate and transmit information, and how these information processes shape firms’ investment decisions and financial reporting outcomes. I am particularly interested in lending relationships, corporate disclosure, and the role of judgment in accounting measurement.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.
